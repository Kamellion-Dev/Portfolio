# Reference analysis and owner-directed scope change

Reference: https://www.padmagnanapriya.com/, inspected on 11 November 2026 at desktop and mobile sizes.

The reference separates About, Skills, Experience, Projects, Writing and Contact. Its career section holds employer responsibilities and results. “Things I’ve built” contains four personal/university projects with compact summaries, stack labels and direct repository links. The hero identifies the person, role, location and stack, then offers contact and CV actions. Serif headings, a full-bleed portrait/video treatment, restrained translucent surfaces and terminal-style details create a cohesive engineering identity.

Useful principles for this portfolio: clear hierarchy, a concise professional introduction, grouped technical capabilities, career evidence kept within employment history, a small personal-project selection and obvious contact/CV links. The reference’s owner-specific claims, metrics, biography and imagery are not reusable content. It also does not prove that excluding commercial projects universally improves recruiting outcomes; this is the owner’s chosen presentation strategy.

Owner steering: remove all employer/client project showcases. Feature only LedgerFlow, Nexus Core, Semsarco and Food Sawa under “Things I’ve built”. Keep commercial responsibilities in work history. The published build must exclude the former commercial case-study pages and screenshots.

Implementation direction: retain Mohamed’s own portrait, blue identity and accessible dark/light presentation. Adapt the concise personal-project structure without cloning the reference’s full-screen imagery, hover-dependent summaries, or unsupported metrics.
